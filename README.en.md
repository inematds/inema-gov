## What it is

INEMA GOV is an applied training program for city halls. Managers, civil servants and the IT team learn to map real processes, simplify routines and build small digital solutions, using artificial intelligence safely. In three months they produce two tested solutions: one for internal work and one for citizens. Here you will find the method, the tracks with open INEMA courses, the templates to fill in and the pilot plan.

## The path

**MAP → IMPROVE → BUILD → VALIDATE → OPERATE → MEASURE AND IMPROVE**

1. **Opening:** two 4-hour talks — leadership (A) and civil servants (B).
2. **Month 1 — learn to transform:** training, mapped processes, two projects chosen.
3. **Month 2 — improve the city hall's work:** one tested internal solution.
4. **Month 3 — make citizens' lives easier:** one solution for the public, tested and, if approved, published.
5. **Months 4 to 12 (annual program):** new cycles, multipliers and a shared library.

## Limits

Goals for planning, not guarantees. No complete systems or complex integrations in 90 days. No automation that decides on its own about rights, benefits, health, taxes or sanctions. The governance points do not replace the legal analysis of each municipality. Institutional project, not partisan.
